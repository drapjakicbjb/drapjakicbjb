Mathematical Foundations of Fractions: Ratios, Decimals & Percentages

A fraction represents a part of a whole or, more generally, any number of equal parts. Expressed in the standard form a/b, the integer above the fraction bar is called the Numerator (indicating how many parts are taken), and the non-zero integer below is the Denominator (indicating the total number of equal parts dividing the unit whole).

1. Types of Mathematical Fractions

  • Proper Fraction: Numerator is strictly less than denominator (\(a < b\)), representing a value less than 1 (e.g., \(3/8 = 0.375\)).
  • Improper Fraction: Numerator is greater than or equal to denominator (\(a \ge b\)), representing a value greater than or equal to 1 (e.g., \(9/4 = 2.25\)).
  • Mixed Number: Combination of a whole integer and a proper fraction (e.g., \(2 \frac{1}{4}\)).

2. Converting Fractions to Decimals and Percentages

Decimal & Percentage Equivalents
Fraction Transformations
$$\text{Decimal} = \frac{a}{b} = a \div b$$ $$\text{Percentage} = \left(\frac{a}{b}\right) \times 100\%$$
\(a\) = Numerator (parts taken)
\(b\) = Denominator (total equal parts)
Condition: Denominator \(b \ne 0\)
Worked Example:
For fraction \(\frac{3}{8}\): $$\text{Decimal} = 3 \div 8 = 0.375$$ $$\text{Percentage} = 0.375 \times 100\% = 37.5\%$$
Fundamental Fraction Arithmetic
Arithmetic Rules
$$\frac{a}{b} \pm \frac{c}{d} = \frac{ad \pm bc}{bd}$$ $$\frac{a}{b} \times \frac{c}{d} = \frac{ac}{bd} \quad \text{and} \quad \frac{a}{b} \div \frac{c}{d} = \frac{ad}{bc}$$
Addition / Subtraction: Find common denominator (\(bd\))
Multiplication: Multiply top by top, bottom by bottom
Division: Multiply by reciprocal of second fraction

3. Frequently Asked Questions (Mathematics)

Q1: What are Equivalent Fractions?

Equivalent fractions represent the same proportion of a whole despite having different numerators and denominators (e.g., \(1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8 = 50\%\)). They are generated by multiplying or dividing both numerator and denominator by the same non-zero integer.
