Institutional Profile & Legacy

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Inter College, located at Belwariya Jungle, Basti, Uttar Pradesh (Pin Code: 272161), is a premier co-educational inter college dedicated to delivering rigorous academic instruction and character development. Established with the vision of honoring Bharat Ratna Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam’s mission for youth empowerment, our college serves hundreds of students across primary, middle, high school, and senior secondary streams.

Our campus combines traditional classroom discipline with modern interactive learning technologies, interactive science simulators, digital portals, and comprehensive academic tools designed to nurture logical reasoning, analytical skills, and competitive readiness.

Vision & Educational Philosophy

Academic Excellence

Providing high-quality instruction in Science, Mathematics, Commerce, and Arts streams with structured curriculum planning aligned with state educational standards.

STEM & Digital Learning

Integrating digital tools, 3D biological models, interactive physics labs, and automated grade calculation systems directly into our student portal.

Digital Innovation & Student Utilities

In line with modern educational requirements, our institution maintains a comprehensive web ecosystem featuring:

  • Interactive Study Hub: Real-time interactive simulators for leaf anatomy, cell biology, wave optics, pendulum harmonics, and solar system orbits.
  • Grade Calculator Portal (GradeCalc): Automated SGPA, CGPA, and percentage conversion engines tailored for high school and university grading standards.
  • Digital Notice Board & Schedules: Instant access to exam timetables, holiday calendars, fee structures, and government scholarship alerts.
