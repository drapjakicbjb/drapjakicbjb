Examination Code of Conduct

Important Rules & Guidelines for Students

All candidates appearing for High School and Intermediate examinations must strictly adhere to the instructions issued by UPMSP Prayagraj.

Admit Card & ID Verification (प्रवेश पत्र)

Every student must carry their original UPMSP Admit Card (प्रवेश पत्र) and School Identity Card to every examination session. Entry into the exam hall will not be permitted without valid verification.

Reporting & Gate Closure Time

Candidates must report to the allocated examination center at least 30 minutes prior to commencement (8:00 AM for Morning Shift & 1:30 PM for Evening Shift). Examination center gates close 15 minutes before the exam.

15-Minute Reading Time (प्रश्न पत्र पठन)

Total duration is 3 hours 15 minutes. The first 15 minutes are strictly dedicated to carefully reading the question paper instructions and verifying subject codes before writing starts.

Class 10 OMR Sheet Rules (20 MCQs)

High School students will receive a 20-mark Objective Section on an OMR Answer Sheet. Use only dark blue or black ballpoint pens. Do not use whitener, eraser, or blade on the OMR sheet.

Permitted Stationery Kit

Only transparent pouches containing blue/black ballpoint pens, HB pencils, eraser, sharpener, and transparent geometry box are permitted. Transparent water bottles are allowed inside the hall.

Strictly Prohibited Electronic Gadgets

Mobile phones, smartwatches, digital wristbands, scientific calculators, Bluetooth earphones, and written slips are strictly banned. Violation will lead to immediate debarment under Anti-Cheating laws.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding UP Board exam schedules and procedures.

The board conducts exams in two shifts: Morning Shift is from 08:30 AM to 11:45 AM (chiefly for Class 10th core papers and select Class 12 papers), while Evening Shift is from 02:00 PM to 05:15 PM (for Class 12th Intermediate papers and select vocational subjects).
Original admit cards stamped and signed by the Principal will be distributed at the school administration office approximately 10 to 15 days prior to the start of the exams. Bring your student ID card or receipt for collection.
No, calculators of any type (simple or scientific) are strictly forbidden in UPMSP High School and Intermediate board examinations. Necessary logarithm tables or data sheets, if required, will be provided by the exam center invigilators.
Notify the school examination coordinator immediately upon receipt of the admit card. The school office will submit a formal correction application to the District Inspector of Schools (DIOS) office before the exam begins.
