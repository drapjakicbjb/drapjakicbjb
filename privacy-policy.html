3. Browser Cookies and Site Data

Our website utilizes browser cookies, local web storage, and caching technologies to provide you with a fast, secure, personalized, and interactive browsing experience.

3.1 What Are Cookies and Site Data?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They help websites remember your actions and preferences over a period of time.

Site Data refers to modern browser-side web storage mechanisms, including:

  • Local Storage (localStorage): Data stored persistently in your browser with no expiration date, remaining available until explicitly cleared.
  • Session Storage (sessionStorage): Transient data stored only for the duration of your active browser tab or session, cleared when the tab is closed.
  • Cache Storage & Service Workers: Local storage managed by our Service Worker script (sw.js) to store essential scripts, stylesheets, and graphics for rapid loading and offline resilience.

3.2 Categories of Cookies & Site Data We Use

We classify the cookies and site data used across our portal into the following categories:

  • Strictly Necessary & Functional Site Data: These are essential for core website navigation, user settings, and interactive educational components.
    • Cookie Consent Preference (kalam_cookie_consent): Stored in your browser's localStorage to remember whether you have acknowledged our privacy and cookie notice, preventing redundant banner prompts on subsequent visits.
    • Interface States (e.g., kalam_wa_floating_minimized): Stored in sessionStorage to preserve user interface adjustments (such as minimized floating tools or assistant widgets) across page navigation without altering layout.
    • Service Worker Cache (apj-school-cache-*): Pre-caches essential stylesheets, fonts, JavaScript libraries, and institutional logos locally via browser Cache Storage to significantly reduce bandwidth usage and ensure fast page load times even on slow internet connections.
    • Virtual Assistant & Interactive Learning Labs: Client-side session storage helps maintain context for our virtual academic guide and STEM simulators (such as physics, optics, biology, and chemistry modules) during your active visit.
  • Performance and Analytics Cookies: We use Google Analytics (gtag.js) and Google Tag Manager to collect anonymous statistical insights into how visitors interact with our portal.
    • These cookies (such as _ga and _ga_*) measure non-personally identifiable metrics, including total visitor counts, popular pages (e.g., UP Board examination schedules, admission guidelines, notice board updates), average time spent on pages, and browser/device types.
    • This analytical data enables our IT and administrative teams to optimize website navigation, resolve broken links, and tailor academic resources to user demand.
  • Third-Party & Advertising Tools: Certain educational notices or contextual banners delivered through Google AdSense may utilize third-party cookies to prevent fraudulent activity, limit repetitive ads, and measure ad viewability in compliance with Google's Advertising Policies.

3.3 Managing and Disabling Cookies & Site Data

**Please note: Disabling essential site data or cookies may prevent certain interactive features (such as offline study tools, preference retention, or virtual assistant interactions) from operating as intended.

You have full control over your browser cookies and stored site data. You can inspect, modify, or block cookies and site data at any time via your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies > See all site data and permissions.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data > Manage Data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies and site data.
  • Mobile Browsers (Android / iOS): Access the browser menu > Settings > Site settings / Privacy > Clear browsing data.
