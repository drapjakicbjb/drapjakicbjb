Payment Guidelines

  • Due Date: Fees must be deposited at the school counter by the 10th of every month.
  • Admission / Registration: New admission registration forms require a one-time processing charge separate from the monthly tuition fee.
  • Test Fees: Collected in advance prior to term examinations and assessments.
