How CGPA to Percentage Works

The general formula for converting CGPA to percentage is:

Percentage (%) = CGPA × Multiplier

For example, with a standard default multiplier of 9.5, a CGPA of 8.40 yields 79.80%. If your institution uses 10, the converted percentage would be 84.00%.
