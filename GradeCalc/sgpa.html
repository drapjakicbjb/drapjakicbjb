How is SGPA Calculated?

SGPA is computed by taking the weighted sum of grade points earned in each subject and dividing it by the sum of credit hours for all subjects taken during that semester.

SGPA = Σ(Credit × Grade Point) / Σ(Credits)

Example Calculation:

Suppose a student took 3 subjects:
• Subject 1: 4 Credits, Grade Point 8 → Credit Points = 4 × 8 = 32
• Subject 2: 3 Credits, Grade Point 9 → Credit Points = 3 × 9 = 27
• Subject 3: 4 Credits, Grade Point 10 → Credit Points = 4 × 10 = 40

Total Credit Points = 32 + 27 + 40 = 99
Total Credits = 4 + 3 + 4 = 11
SGPA = 99 / 11 = 9.00
