About GradeCalc

GradeCalc is a modern, student-centric academic calculator platform created to simplify SGPA, CGPA, and percentage calculations.

Our Mission

Understanding academic grade point averages should be simple, transparent, and error-free. GradeCalc was built to eliminate manual calculation mistakes by providing clean, responsive, and instant calculation tools for students across schools, colleges, and universities.

Privacy & Client-Side Processing

We take student data privacy seriously. GradeCalc functions entirely inside your local web browser using client-side JavaScript.

  • Zero server requests or database storage for entered grades.
  • No cookies, tracking scripts, or analytical trackers.
  • Works offline once loaded in your browser.

Mathematical Accuracy & Standards

GradeCalc implements exact credit-weighted mathematical formulas:

SGPA = Σ(Credit × Grade Point) / Σ(Credit)
CGPA = Σ(SGPA × Semester Credits) / Σ(Semester Credits)

Calculations maintain full floating-point precision throughout intermediate steps and are rounded to 2 decimal places only when rendering the final output.

Disclaimer: GradeCalc is an independent educational tool. Academic grading criteria and percentage conversion formulas vary across educational boards and universities. Please consult your institution's official academic office for formal evaluations.
