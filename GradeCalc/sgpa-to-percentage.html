How SGPA to Percentage Works

Most Indian and international universities convert SGPA into marks percentage using a direct multiplier. The standard default formula is:

Percentage (%) = SGPA × 9.5

For instance, an SGPA of 8.50 multiplied by 9.5 equals 80.75%. If your institution uses another multiplier (like 10), select Custom University Multiplier above.

SGPA Conversion FAQs

Different educational boards (such as CBSE or specific technical universities) design their grade distributions according to distinct statistical curves. Always consult your official university grade transcript back page for exact rules.
