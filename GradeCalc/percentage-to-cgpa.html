How Percentage to CGPA Conversion Works

Converting percentage marks back into CGPA uses the inverse of the percentage formula:

CGPA = Percentage (%) / Multiplier

For example, a percentage of 85.50% divided by a standard multiplier of 9.5 equals an estimated CGPA of 9.00.
