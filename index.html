Abdul Kalam Inter College, situated in Belwariya Jungle, Basti, Uttar Pradesh, was founded with the deeply inspired belief that quality education is the cornerstone of personal and societal transformation. Named after India's esteemed "Missile Man" and former President, our institution honors his vision by cultivating discipline, scientific curiosity, and a love for lifelong learning.

We offer a safe, modern, and intellectually stimulating ecosystem designed to support students from early childhood foundations up to higher secondary examinations, ensuring they build strong academic roots and develop active creative minds.

Academics

Our Academic Programmes

Comprehensive academic pathways designed to ignite intellectual curiosity, establish robust concepts, and guide students toward stellar achievements.

Primary School

Nursery to Class V. A warm and nurturing environment centered on activity-based, play-focused pedagogy. We lay strong foundational blocks in early literacy, mathematical reasoning, and creative expression.

Middle School

Class VI to VIII. Bridging foundational learning with structured concepts. Students explore specialized fields in languages, natural sciences, mathematics, and social sciences, developing analytical thinking skills.

Secondary School

Class IX to X (UP Board). A comprehensive and rigorous curriculum focused on lab-based sciences, advanced mathematics, and linguistic proficiency, laying the groundwork for board exam success.

Higher School

Class XI to XII (UP Board). Streams: Science (PCM / PCB) and Arts. We provide specialized guidance, modern laboratory experimentation, and coaching designed to ensure outstanding performance in board exams and future competitive entrances.

Co-curricular Activities

A rich array of cultural arts, dynamic debates, competitive sports, and leadership programs to cultivate multi-faceted skills, physical health, and teamwork.
